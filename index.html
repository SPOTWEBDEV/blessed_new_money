The person behind the brand

Ezechi Callistus Mmadubuchi

Founder & CEO, Blessed New Money Fashion King

Callistus started out grading bales on the floor of Onitsha Main Market and built Blessed New Money Fashion King into a trusted name for traders and personal buyers across Nigeria. His approach is simple: grade honestly, price fairly, and treat a single-piece buyer the same way as a full bulk wholesaler. "With God all things are possible" isn't just a tagline here — it's how the business is run.
